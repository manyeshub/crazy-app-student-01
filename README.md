## ⚡ Catchy Headline: Ignite the Digital Cosmos with Neon Light & Pure Synthesizer Frequencies!

**CYBERPULSE** is a futuristic, single-file interactive cyberpunk particle physics sandbox and generative Web Audio synth engine running entirely client-side in the browser at a silky smooth 60 FPS. Every swipe, drag, and click detonates luminous neon shockwaves, particle cascades, and algorithmic pentatonic synthesizer melodies.

---

## 🎮 How to Play / Lab Controls

| Action | Control | What Happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Neon Stream** | Drag Mouse / Swipe Touch | Emits high-velocity glowing cyberpunk particles following your motion |
| **Nova Explosion** | Left Click / Tap | Detonates a 70-particle blast with expanding neon shockwave & synth burst |
| **Black Hole / Gravity Well** | Hold Right Click | Inverts physics into a singularity, pulling all active particles toward your cursor |
| **EMP Shockwave Blast** | Spacebar / "EMP Blast" Button | Fires a massive 220-particle full-screen cosmic detonation with sub-bass chord |
| **Hyperspace Warp Drive** | Key `W` / "Warp Mode" Button | Accelerates all particles beyond light speed with trail distortion |
| **Palette Shifter** | Control Dock Buttons | Swap between *Cyberpunk*, *Matrix*, *Solar*, and *Abyss* neon color spectrums |
| **Audio Synthesizer** | "Audio: ON" / "Bass Drone" | Toggle realtime Web Audio oscillators and hypnotic sub-bass drone |

## 🕵️‍♂️ Secret Features

1. **Hyperspace Overdrive (Press `W`):** Unlocks an exponential velocity multiplier that warps particle trajectories into cosmic light rays while triggering a glowing HUD banner.
2. **Singularity Gravity Well (Right Click):** Turns your cursor into an interactive gravitational attractor, allowing you to orbit and slingshot particle galaxies.
3. **Harmonic Pentatonic Melodies:** The Web Audio synthesizer dynamically maps your cursor's X position to notes in the Cyberpunk Pentatonic scale ($C_3$ to $A_5$) and modulates the lowpass filter cutoff with the Y position.

It pushes each ball forward based on its roller-coaster speed (velocity), gently pulls it down toward the floor (gravity), slowly cools down its bright neon flashlight (fading opacity and life), and when a ball runs out of juice, the referee throws it away into the cosmic recycling bin so new shiny balls can jump in!"*
